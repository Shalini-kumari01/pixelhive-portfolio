About PIXELHIVEE

PIXELHIVEE is a social media management and creative media brand. The work covers planning and posting content, shooting photos and videos, and editing short-form videos for social platforms.

The goal is simple: help your brand look consistent and show up well online, with content that supports your growth.

Services

Social Media Management

Planning, scheduling and managing your social media presence so your page stays active and on brand.

Content Creation

Posts, graphics and captions made to fit your brand and your audience.

Photography

Photos for your products, brand and social media feed.

Videography

Video shooting and editing for brands, promotions and social media.

Reels and Short-form Video

Vertical videos made for Instagram Reels and other short-form platforms.
